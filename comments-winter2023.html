  4. If you could give one piece of advice to someone taking this course in the future, what would it be?
    • Because all the assignment requirement documents are already present on the website in advance. I recommend that they review the requirements section of each assignment in full in advance. This will give you a complete view of the entire project. It also allows students to determine in advance which parts should be modularized. This will greatly increase their productivity.
    • Make sure your classes are solid! As you will use them the entire term.
    • The advice that I would like to give to someone who is taking this course in future would be to start the projects well before their deadline so that they can get enough time to test the various scenarios.  Also, I would advice them to go through the topic videos and notes in their respective weeks so that it would be helpful for them in quizzes as well as projects.
    • Use test-driven development and write your tests well.  In my experience, I did know anything about testing before taking this class.  My tests were not the greatest.   It will be a good idea to write good test and start off strong.
    • Get a big head-start on Koans -- try to finish it all before Project 2. It will help you understand how to write strong tests. 
    • Start all the projects at least a week before they are due.
    • Make sure your schedule allows for taking this class as it has a heavier workload
    • Front-load your work. Get the first projects working flawlessly in order to avoid the later projects becoming a full-time job.
    • I would says don't wait up for last minute to work on projects. Try to attend the lectures in synchronous mode as it helps in asking questions and getting them immediately resolved compared to watching lecture videos later.  Participate in Slack. Class is super interactive, most of the problems that we come across in assignments can be solved via communicating with classmates going through similar issues.
    • START ASSIGNMENTS EARLY, ASK A TON OF QUESTIONS.
